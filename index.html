Good to know

Frequently Asked

What is the dress code?

Our dress code is in the Dress Code section: strictly formal, with suits and ties for gentlemen and floor-length evening gowns for ladies. We kindly invite you to review the recommended attire and color palette before the wedding day. Please note that white and beige are reserved exclusively for the couple, and we sincerely appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

Can I bring a plus one?

Unfortunately, no. Due to limited seating and venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate guests whose names are specifically listed on the invitation. We kindly ask for your understanding and request that no additional guests be brought to the wedding. Thank you for helping us celebrate within our planned arrangements.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony starts at exactly 4:00 PM. We kindly ask all guests to be at the venue by 3:30 PM so everyone is settled before we begin.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Kindly submit your RSVP on or before November 25, 2026. Your response is greatly appreciated, as it helps us finalize our guest count, seating arrangements, catering, and other important wedding details.

Can I bring my baby or kids?

To keep the celebration intimate and peaceful, we respectfully ask that this be an adults-only celebration. We hope you understand.

What can I give the couple as a gift?

Your presence is our greatest gift. Should you wish to give, we gratefully prefer monetary gifts. You'll find our details in the Gift Guide.
